\title{Proof of the Pythagorean Theorem}

\section*{Proof}

Consider a right triangle with legs of length \(a\) and \(b\), and hypotenuse \(c\).

We can construct a square with side length \(a + b\) and place four copies of the
right triangle inside it, each copy a quarter turn from the last, so that along
every side of the large square a leg of length \(a\) is followed by a leg of
length \(b\):

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.48]
  % the large square, side a + b (drawn with a = 3, b = 4, c = 5)
  \draw[thick] (0,0) rectangle (7,7);
  % four copies of the triangle, each rotated a quarter turn from the last
  \fill[black!12] (3,0) -- (7,0) -- (7,3) -- cycle;
  \fill[black!12] (7,3) -- (7,7) -- (4,7) -- cycle;
  \fill[black!12] (4,7) -- (0,7) -- (0,4) -- cycle;
  \fill[black!12] (0,4) -- (0,0) -- (3,0) -- cycle;
  % the square left in the middle, each side a hypotenuse
  \draw[thick] (3,0) -- (7,3) -- (4,7) -- (0,4) -- cycle;
  \node[below] at (1.5,0) {\(a\)};
  \node[below] at (5,0)   {\(b\)};
  \node[right] at (7,1.5) {\(a\)};
  \node[right] at (7,5)   {\(b\)};
  \node at (5.35,1.05) {\(c\)};
  \node at (3.5,3.5) {\(c^2\)};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

The area of the large square is \((a + b)^2\).

The four right triangles each have an area of \(\frac{1}{2}ab\).

The quadrilateral left in the center has all four sides equal to \(c\), because
each side is the hypotenuse of one copy. Its corners are right angles: where two
copies meet on a side of the large square, that straight angle is made up of the
two acute angles \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) of the triangle together with the
corner of the quadrilateral, and \(\alpha + \beta = 90^\circ\) since they are the
acute angles of a right triangle. The center is therefore a square, and its area
is \(c^2\).

Thus, we have:

\[
(a + b)^2 = 4 \left( \frac{1}{2} ab \right) + c^2
\]

Simplifying, we get:

\[
a^2 + 2ab + b^2 = 2ab + c^2
\]

Subtracting \(2ab\) from both sides, we obtain:

\[
a^2 + b^2 = c^2
\]

This completes the proof of the Pythagorean theorem.
